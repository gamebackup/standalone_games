# Data notice

`data.js` is a derived, merged answer bank for this offline tribute. It is built from two public Krillion reveal datasets.

## Microhx/krillion-answers

Source: <https://github.com/Microhx/krillion-answers>

> - **Not official.** This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the publisher of Krillion.
> - **Source.** Values are derived from publicly reachable reveal data at `krillion.io`. Only data that the publisher made publicly available at some point is included.
> - **Rights.** Prompt wording, answer lists, rarity tiers, scores and any descriptive text remain the property of their respective owner. This repository claims no ownership over them.
> - **Scope.** The published dataset intentionally excludes the publisher's authored "quip" descriptions. Only short factual fields are distributed: mode, date/game id, round, prompt, answer, tier, score, category.
> - **Purpose.** Reference, research and personal tooling. Do not use it to build a competing product or to misrepresent the official game.
> - **Take-down.** If you are the rights holder and want content removed, open an issue in that repository and it will be removed promptly.
>
> If you redistribute this dataset, keep this notice with it.

## KrillionHQ / StruggleYang krillion-dataset

Source: <https://github.com/StruggleYang/krillion-dataset> — **CC BY 4.0**.

> KrillionHQ (2026). *Krillion Daily Dive Dataset* — prompts and rarity-tiered answers for every Krillion daily dive.

The merge keeps the most common grade where an answer appeared with different grades, drops answers over 100 characters and entries without letters or digits, and invents no new grades. Prompts, answers and grades remain the property of their respective owner. This offline game is not affiliated with Daily, playdaily.org, or the original Krillion.
