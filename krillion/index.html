How to play+

Each prompt has one open question and a bank of accepted answers. Type an answer and press Enter. Your first accepted answer ends the prompt, so reach for the rarest one you are sure of.

Answers are graded by how likely people are to give them. Every point sinks the krill 10 metres. Speed earns nothing — the clock only decides whether you answer at all. A refusal costs only time: try again until the clock runs out.

The bank holds — accepted answers across — prompts. Small typos, plurals and missing punctuation are forgiven. Answers outside the bank are refused — there is no AI and no crowd, so the bank is the whole ocean.

This is an unofficial offline tribute. Prompts and answer grades are built from publicly released Krillion reveal data; it is not affiliated with Daily or the original game.
